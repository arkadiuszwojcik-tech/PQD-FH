# About the author — Arkadiusz Wójcik

**Electrical & Automation Engineer | E&I QA/QS Manager | Commissioning**

Arkadiusz Wójcik works in industrial electrical engineering, automation, quality assurance and commissioning. His engineering interests include PLC systems, electrical and instrumentation inspection, test-record verification, documentation traceability and lifecycle information.

He develops the **PQD-FH — Progressive Quality Documentation & Final Handover Framework**, a publicly available approach to controlling quality records and technical handover progressively throughout project execution.

He is also developing **UnitedQMS**, a separate documentation-focused quality management software initiative. Its design explores traceable workflows, controlled AI-supported verification, hybrid/local AI processing and human approvals. UnitedQMS is in development; its software is not a published part of the PQD-FH documentation repository.

## Engineering areas

- Electrical and instrumentation QA/QS, inspections and commissioning
- PLC engineering, I/O verification and industrial communications
- Inspection and Test Programmes / Plans (ITP / ITPL)
- Red Line Mark-Up, as-built documentation and final quality dossiers
- Version control, record verification and engineering-data traceability
